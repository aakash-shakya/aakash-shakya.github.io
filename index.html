From first chat to finished.

The same four stops every time. Follow the line down — it fills in as you travel.

  1. We talk

    One relaxed conversation about what you need and what it should do. I write it back in plain words — if it's not clear, we don't start.

    DAY 1 · FREE

  2. You see it

    How it will look and work, sketched together — never separately. You click through an early version and tell me what's wrong while it's still cheap to fix.
